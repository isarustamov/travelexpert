# TravelExpert AI Product Requirements

## Vision
Build a premium AI-powered travel comparison SaaS that combines flights, hotels, transfers, insurance, visa guidance, weather, food, local transport, and full-cost package recommendations. The first target market is Azerbaijan and nearby countries, with SEO landing pages for high-intent routes.

## MVP Scope
- Home search page with premium hero, trust badges, content blocks, FAQs, and CTA.
- Mock flight, hotel, and transfer providers behind replaceable service modules.
- Package builder producing cheapest, best-value, fastest, premium, and family packages.
- Total trip cost calculator including insurance, food, local transport, visa fee, service fee, per-person cost, and budget delta.
- AI recommendation copy generated from supplied mock data and clearly marked as estimated.
- Search results page with tabs, filters, package cards, risk notes, price breakdown, save/share/alert UI hooks.
- Local/in-memory saved-trip API placeholder and Prisma schema for database persistence.

## Personas
- Budget travelers seeking cheapest viable complete trip.
- Families prioritizing baggage, cancellation, location, and transfers.
- Business travelers prioritizing direct flights and reliable transfers.
- Students seeking low-cost weekends.
- Tourists from Azerbaijan and nearby countries who need total trip visibility.

## Success Metrics
- Search-to-package-view conversion.
- Save-trip and price-alert creation rate.
- Affiliate click-through rate by provider and package type.
- Premium subscription conversion for alerts, PDF export, and AI concierge.
- SEO landing page sessions and route-intent rankings.
