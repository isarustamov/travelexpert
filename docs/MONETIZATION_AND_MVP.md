# Monetization Strategy and MVP Plan

## Monetization
- Flight, hotel, insurance, and transfer affiliate links.
- Sponsored hotel placements with clear labels.
- Premium subscription for advanced alerts and PDF itinerary export.
- Paid AI concierge for complex trips.
- B2B agency panel and lead generation.
- Commission management by provider and package type.

## MVP Delivery Steps
1. Ship responsive home and search pages.
2. Replace mock data with provider adapter interfaces.
3. Persist searches, packages, saved trips, and alerts in PostgreSQL.
4. Add auth and user dashboard.
5. Add AI recommendation endpoint and prompt management.
6. Add affiliate click tracking.
7. Add SEO CMS and destination pages.
8. Add subscriptions and premium exports.
